Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy explains how Pit & Pitch ("we", "us") handles information. Pit & Pitch is a YouTube channel run by Sabavat Harinath. It covers this website and the "Pit and Pitch Autopilot" application, a private tool that we use only to manage our own YouTube channel.

This website

This website does not use accounts, forms, cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and we do not collect personal information through it. The page loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of a normal web request (see Google's privacy policy below). The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs.

Pit and Pitch Autopilot (YouTube API)

Pit and Pitch Autopilot uses the YouTube API Services. It is used only by the channel owner, on the channel owner's own computer, to manage the Pit & Pitch channel. It is not offered to the public.

  • What it accesses: with the channel owner's permission, it creates, updates, starts and ends live broadcasts on the owner's own YouTube channel, and sets their titles, descriptions and thumbnails.
  • What it stores: the access token Google issues for the owner's account, saved only on the owner's computer. It does not collect, store or share data about any other YouTube user or viewer.
  • Sharing: we do not sell, rent or share any data obtained through the YouTube API Services with anyone.
  • Revoking access: access can be removed at any time from the Google security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Deleting the token file on the owner's computer also removes it.

By using the YouTube API Services, this application is bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

YouTube channel

When you watch our videos or live streams, comment or chat, that happens on YouTube and is governed by YouTube's Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. We can see the public information YouTube shows to every channel owner (for example public comments and chat messages, and aggregated, anonymous analytics). We do not export or sell it.

Children

This website and our application are not directed to children and do not knowingly collect information from anyone.
